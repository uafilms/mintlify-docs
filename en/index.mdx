## What is UAFilms?

UAFilms is a high-performance backend aggregator for Ukrainian media content and a streaming server built on top of the [OMSS (Open Media Streaming Specification)](https://github.com/omss-spec/omss-spec).

It unifies popular Ukrainian online streaming platforms and CDNs into a single, blazing-fast, and reliable API for catalog discovery, metadata, and video streaming.

## Quickstart

1. Register or log in to your account at [films.aartzz.pp.ua](https://films.aartzz.pp.ua).
2. Go to **Settings** ➔ **Account** ➔ **API** or open the [Developer Dashboard](https://films.aartzz.pp.ua/dashboard).
3. Generate your personal API key (`uaf_live_...`) and send it in the `x-api-key` header.
